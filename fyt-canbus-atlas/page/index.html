How a canbox frame becomes a value on screen

Canbox → MCUUART frames, e.g. Raise 2E 41 0D 02 …
Decoder in com.syu.ms807 classes. Picked by profile id in kt.h0(). V2(bytes) turns bytes into codes with kt.w0(code, value)
Parser in com.syu.canbus640 classes, Callback_NNNN_…. Picked in HandlerCanbus. Names the codes (U_CUR_SPEED = 109)
Screens and BilDoors, climate, trip pages. Bil reads the same codes from module 7

A profile id is carId << 16 | canbusId. Your profile decodes with module/canbus/c and names with Callback_0051_RZC_DaZhong (Raise, VW PQ).

Findings

You are on Raise VW PQ (0051): 91 signals in 8 sensor categories, rank 34 of 652

Only this decoder produces the codes Bil receives (104 fuel, 106 odometer, 109 speed, 110 rpm, 101 seatbelt), so the profile is settled. Its frames:

  • Sent by your firmware: 0x20 keys, 0x21 climate, 0x22–0x25 radar and parking, 0x41/0x01 doors, seatbelt, washer fluid and handbrake, 0x41/0x02 rpm, speed, battery voltage, outside temperature, odometer and fuel, 0x41/0x03 low fuel and low voltage.
  • Accepted but never sent: 0x41/0x11, /0x12, /0x13 and /0x04 (car-info fields FYT only names by byte position, likely the trip computer and dashboard pages). The rest are car-settings pages: 0x27, 0x41/0x40, /0x80, /0x81 and /0xA8.
  • No TPMS, lights or clock in this protocol at all.

The Volvo XC90 profiles are among the thinnest

Raise XC90 → 0452_RZC_Volvo_XC60 (22 signals: doors, climate, speed). HiWorld XC90 → 0444_WC_Volvo_All (22). OD and LZ XC90 → doors only (5). Switching to a Volvo profile would lose what you have now.

The most realistic upgrade: Raise Toyota "ALL 2013" (0020)

131 signals in 10 sensor categories, and your firmware already has a Raise Toyota mode (same 2E framing, radar 0x1E and base info 0x24 are in this decoder too). It adds what VW PQ lacks:

  • TPMS on 0x25: four pressures plus status (codes 107–114)
  • Trip computer: 0x21 average speed, driving time and trip distance; 0x22 current consumption; 0x23 trip consumption
  • Lights and warnings on 0x26 and 0x41, separate seatbelt left/right on 0x24, rpm and speed on 0x35

The cost: rewriting the vehicle frames to Toyota's layout, and the head unit's screens become the Toyota ones.

Method and limits

  • Sources: /odm/app/190000000_com.syu.canbus, com.syu.ms and the profile database canbus.db (in ProtocolUpdate.apk), pulled read-only from the DUDU7 on 2026-10-14 and decompiled with jadx.
  • Profile → protocol: by interpreting the Dalvik bytecode of FYT's selection methods for every id. jadx's Java for HandlerCanbus was wrong for 1,008 profiles (switch fall-through), so it was not used.
  • Byte layout: read from the decoders' V2() text. B[0] is the command byte, B[1] the length, B[2]… data (for Raise; some makers test B[1] as the command). 84 % of writes have their frame identified; the rest sit in helper methods.
  • Names: from the parser's U_* constants, FYT's shared climate slots and the door slots. Categories come from keywords in those names and can misfile a few.
  • Not covered: key codes beyond each decoder's key table, values sent from the head unit to the car (settings changes), and whether a given car actually puts the data on CAN.
